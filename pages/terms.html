Please read these terms before preparing a service enquiry.

Service information

This website provides electrical-service information and a frontend enquiry planning tool. It does not itself dispatch an electrician or confirm an appointment.

Pricing guidance

Displayed rates are illustrative starting labour prices. Site conditions, materials and approved scope determine the provider’s final quote. No price is binding until agreed with the provider.

Appointments and coverage

Preferred dates and time windows are requests. Service area coverage, availability and all visit arrangements must be confirmed directly with your chosen provider.

Safety and professional work

Website content is general service-planning information. Electrical work requires appropriate qualifications and an assessment of the specific installation. Do not use these pages as repair instructions.

External handoff

Copying or downloading a request does not send it. You are responsible for reviewing the details and sharing the request with your provider. Their service terms apply to any work you arrange.
