How this frontend enquiry website uses information.

The details you enter

The booking form uses your name, contact information, service needs and location to prepare a request summary in your browser. There is no connected booking backend. Your details are not submitted by this site.

Local preferences

Theme and reading-direction preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. The form details are held in the current page session and are not saved to local storage.

Copying and downloading

Copying places the request on your device clipboard. Downloading saves a text file on your device. You control who receives these details when you share them outside the website.

Images, fonts and analytics

Images and fonts are served from the website’s own files. No analytics or advertising scripts are included. Your hosting provider may process standard access logs under its own policies.

Requests about your information

For enquiries shared outside this site, contact the recipient directly about access, corrections or deletion. If enquiry sending becomes available, review the updated privacy notice before sharing personal details.
