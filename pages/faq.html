FAQ

A little clarity goes a long way.

Useful answers about service requests, inspection, pricing and planning your visit.

What electrical services can I enquire about?+

Home wiring, fan installation, light fitting, switchboard and socket repairs, electrical inspections and panel upgrades. Describe your needs in the enquiry form so the scope can be reviewed.

How does the pricing guide work?+

The displayed starting prices are illustrative design rates. The actual quote depends on the inspection, site conditions, labour, materials and access. Agree the scope and total price before work begins.

Are materials included?+

Replacement fittings, cables, protection devices and other materials are not included in the indicative labour rates unless an individual quote explicitly includes them.

Can I request a preferred date and time?+

Yes. Add your preference to your enquiry. A requested time is not a confirmed appointment; availability must be agreed directly with the service provider.

What happens after I prepare an enquiry?+

Review your request, then copy or download it and share it with your chosen provider. This website does not send requests or arrange visits automatically.

What should I do if I notice exposed wiring or sparks?+

Do not touch damaged equipment or exposed wiring. Keep people away. If there is immediate danger, move away and contact the appropriate local emergency service or a qualified electrical professional.

Can I request an electrical inspection?+

Yes. Choose Inspection & Troubleshooting and describe concerns such as repeated tripping, damaged accessories or an older installation. Inspection findings determine the recommended work.

How are panel upgrades assessed?+

A qualified electrician needs to assess the existing installation, household load, circuit protection, earthing and available space. The suitable scope and price follow that assessment.
