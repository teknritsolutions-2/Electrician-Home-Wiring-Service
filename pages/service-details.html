Home Wiring

A well-connected home starts with the wiring behind your walls.

THOUGHTFUL WORK, CLEARLY EXPLAINED

When to consider this service

New rooms, renovations, ageing wiring or recurring faults can all call for a professional wiring assessment.

What the electrician checks

  • Condition and suitability of existing wiring
  • Circuit requirements, connections and protection
  • Access and the proposed route for new cables

Typical scope

Inspection, agreed cable installation or replacement, appropriate connections, testing and verification. The final scope depends on the property assessment.

Keep safety at the centre.

Do not attempt live wiring work or open electrical assemblies. Arrange assessment and work by a qualified electrician. Damaged wiring should be kept out of reach.

Before the visit

Note the affected rooms, describe when the issue occurs and share any renovation plans. Agree access, credentials, materials and the quote with your provider.
