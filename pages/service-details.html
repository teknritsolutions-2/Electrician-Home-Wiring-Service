01 / SERVICE SPECIFICATION

A clear scope, before work begins.

When to consider it

New rooms, renovations, ageing wiring or recurring faults can call for a professional assessment.

The typical scope

Inspection, agreed installation or replacement, appropriate connections, testing and verification.

02 / INSTALLATION CONTEXT

What the electrician checks.

Attention to the existing installation, suitable equipment and the work your home actually needs.

ASSESSMENT CHECKLIST
  • Condition and suitability of existing wiring
  • Circuit requirements, connections and protection
  • Access and the proposed route for new cables

The assessment determines the final scope. Related work and materials are agreed separately.

03 / BEFORE THE VISIT

Make room for a considered assessment.

A few useful details

  1. Note the affected rooms, symptoms and when the issue occurs.
  2. Share renovation plans and details of proposed fixtures or equipment.
  3. Agree access, credentials, materials and the quote with your provider.

Keep safety at the centre.

Do not attempt live wiring work or open electrical assemblies. Arrange assessment and work by a qualified electrician.
